## Try reactions

Open a highlighted discussion. Each comment shows its reactions under the text. Your own reactions are pressed and tinted blue; other people's are plain. Hover or focus a chip to see who reacted. Click a chip to add or remove your reaction, or use the smiley button to pick one of six reactions.

Reactions are a quiet acknowledgement. They never send a notification, and agents can read them but not add them.

## Decision

Keep invitation links valid for seven days, then let the inviter send a fresh link from the members page.

## Reminders

Send one reminder email after three days if the invitation is still unused. Skip the reminder when the workspace admin has already revoked the invitation.
