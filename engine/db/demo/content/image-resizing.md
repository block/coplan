Open **Edit**, then hover over either image to reveal its bottom-right resize handle. Drag inward and release to save a smaller width. Grab the handle again and drag near the full width: the image snaps back, shows **100%**, and removes the width setting when released.

<img src="{{demo_logo_url}}" alt="CoPlan logo at a saved width" width="320">

Both images keep their proportions. **Undo** reverses one resize at a time. Press **Escape** during a drag to cancel. With the handle focused, arrow keys resize, **Home** chooses the smallest size, and **End** restores full width. **Dual** shows the Markdown changing when you release the handle.
