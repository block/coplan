## User problem

A workspace admin needs to invite a teammate and know whether the invitation can still be accepted.

## Requirements

- An admin can invite one email address at a time.
- The recipient sees the workspace name and granted role before accepting.
- Retried delivery never creates a second membership.
- Expired invitations explain how to request a new one.

## Success measures

Measure invitation acceptance, delivery latency, and duplicate membership attempts for the beta cohort. A successful launch has no duplicate memberships and no unexplained acceptance errors.

## Out of scope

Bulk invitations and domain-wide automatic enrollment follow a separate proposal.
