## Working hypothesis

New teams activate when the first document gets a useful reply. The empty workspace should guide an author toward inviting one reviewer rather than creating more folders.

## Questions

- How quickly does a new author receive their first reply?
- Can we suggest a reviewer without exposing private drafts?
- What should the workspace show before any document exists?
