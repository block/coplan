## The opportunity

Review feedback arrives after implementation has started. A small, visible review queue can bring the right people into a plan while decisions are still inexpensive to change.

## Launch criteria

| Signal | Target | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time to first response | Under one working day | Engineering lead |
| Reviewers who return | At least 60% | Product |
| Unanswered questions | None at rollout | Author |
