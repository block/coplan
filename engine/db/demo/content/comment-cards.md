## Summary

Readers lose track of a review when every discussion collapses into a small marker. This design shows each open discussion as a card in the right margin, aligned with the passage it quotes, so reviewers can read the document and the conversation side by side.

Cards keep the document readable. The margin holds the starter, the first comment, and a short reply summary; the full thread opens on demand.

## Placement

Each card starts level with its highlighted passage. When two highlights sit close together, the card for the earlier passage keeps its place and the later card is pushed down until it clears the one above. The next line holds two discussions side by side, so their cards stack.

The rollout touches the cache warmup and the retry budget in a single release.

Cards never overlap. When the stack grows long, later cards drift further from their text. Opening a card lines it up with its passage again and moves the cards above it up to make room. Hovering a card or its passage lights up the other, so readers never need a thin connector to see which passage a card discusses.

## Content

A card shows the first comment in full when it is short. Longer comments are truncated after a few lines and end with more. Clicking a card expands it in place into the full discussion, with reply and resolve.

Replies are summarized by count and the most recent participant, so a busy discussion stays compact until someone opens it.

## Lifecycle

Resolved discussions are hidden by default, which keeps the margin focused on open questions. Show resolved brings them back as dimmed cards in their original positions.

Comments that are not attached to any passage remain in the Comments section at the end of the document.

## Narrow windows

Cards need room beside the text. On windows narrower than about 1,650 pixels the margin falls back to compact markers, and the reader's choice is remembered for the next wide window.
